Getting there

By air: O.R. Tambo International Airport, next to Kempton Park.
By rail: Gautrain links to Johannesburg and Pretoria.
By road / ride-hailing: Ride-hailing and hotel shuttles cover local travel.
Address: 64 Jones Road, Kempton Park, Johannesburg.
